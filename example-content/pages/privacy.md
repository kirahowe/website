## Email subscriptions

If you subscribe by email, your address is sent to Buttondown and used to
deliver updates from this site. I won't use it for anything else, and every
email includes a link to unsubscribe.

## Analytics and server logs

This site uses a self-hosted Umami installation to understand aggregate site
traffic. It doesn't use advertising cookies or build a profile about you.
Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure may also process basic
technical information, such as your IP address and browser details, in its
server logs.
